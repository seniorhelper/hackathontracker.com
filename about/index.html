About Hackathon Tracker

Hackathon Tracker exists to answer one question: which hackathon is worth entering?

Listings are scattered across a dozen platforms, most lists are sorted by date, and none of them tell you how crowded an event is compared with its prize money. So this site pulls the public listings into one search, works out the prize per entrant where the numbers exist, and lets you filter down to the ones that fit you, whether you are a student, a working adult or somewhere in between.

It is free, there is no account, and every listing links straight to the organizer’s page. We are independent and not affiliated with any platform or organizer listed here.
