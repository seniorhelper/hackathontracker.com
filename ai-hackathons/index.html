Picking an AI hackathon

Many AI hackathons are run by a company that wants people building on its model or platform. Read the rules for which tools are required, because a great project on the wrong stack is not eligible. Prizes are sometimes paid partly in platform credits rather than cash, so look at the prize breakdown on the official page before you commit.

An event counts as AI here when its title or topic tags mention AI, machine learning, LLMs, agents or a major model by name.
