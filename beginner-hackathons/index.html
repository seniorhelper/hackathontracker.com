First hackathon?

Pick one with a deadline at least two weeks out, a topic you already care about, and rules you can read in five minutes. You do not need a team to start. A small working demo with a clear two-minute video beats an ambitious idea that does not run.

We only show the beginner tag when the organizer set it themselves. Plenty of untagged events welcome first-timers too, so it is worth browsing online hackathons as well.
