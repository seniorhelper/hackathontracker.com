Why sort hackathons by odds?

Most lists sort by date. That tells you when a hackathon ends, not whether it is worth entering. Two events can offer the same prize money while one has a few hundred people signed up and the other has tens of thousands. Your chances are not the same, and no date sort will show you that.

So where a listing gives us both numbers, we divide the cash prize by the number of people signed up and show it on the card as prize per entrant. Right now 56 of the 405 hackathons here have enough data to score. The top third offer $60 or more per entrant and get a “Strong odds” badge. What you can do here

  • Search everything at once. Listings are pulled from several hackathon platforms and duplicates are merged, with a link back to the original page.
  • Filter to what fits you. Online or in person, a city or country, minimum prize, deadline window, beginner friendly, AI, or hide events that are for students only.
  • Keep a shortlist. Hit Save on any card. Your list stays in your browser. There is no account and nothing to sign up for.
  • Never miss a deadline. Add any deadline to your calendar in one click, or subscribe to the feeds to hear about new listings.
