Online vs. in person

Online hackathons usually give you more time and bigger prize pools, but they also draw bigger crowds because anyone in the world can enter. That is where the odds column earns its keep: sort by “Best odds first” or “Fewest competitors” to find the quieter ones.

If meeting people is the main goal, an event in a room still beats a Discord server. Try the in-person list and type your city.
