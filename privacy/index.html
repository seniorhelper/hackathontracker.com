Privacy

Short version: we do not collect anything about you.

  • No accounts and no forms. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No cookies, analytics or ad trackers are set by this site.
  • Your saved list is stored in your own browser. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Near me asks your browser for your location only when you press the button. The distance sums happen on your device and your location is never sent to us or put in the page address.
  • Links to hackathon pages go to other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

The site is served by a hosting provider that, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. If this page changes, the change will be posted here.
