Privacy Policy

Last updated Nov 20, 2026.

This policy explains what information is collected when you use hackathontracker.com (“the site”), how it is used, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • No accounts. You can search, filter and save hackathons without signing up or giving us your name or email.
  • Forms. If you post a project to the entry wall or use the contact form, what you type is sent to our own secure lead server, which stores it and notifies us. No third-party form service is used. We use it only to review your entry or answer you. For entry-wall posts we publish only the project details you chose to share. Your email address is never published. Ask us at any time to remove your entry or delete your message.
  • Your saved list is stored in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Near me asks your browser for your location only when you press the button. The distance math happens on your device, and your location is never sent to us or put in the page address.
  • Server logs. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard request logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

Advertising and cookies

The site shows ads served by Google AdSense, which helps keep it free. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com).
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info (US) or youronlinechoices.eu (EU).
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and How Google uses cookies in advertising.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Ads may then be less relevant, but the site still works.

We do not set our own analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal information.

Links to other sites

Hackathon listings link to organizers’ and platforms’ websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Children

The site is a general-audience directory and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to how it is used. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
